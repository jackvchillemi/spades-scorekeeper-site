Spades Scorekeeper

Keep score at Spades, minus the paper.

Spades Scorekeeper is a free iPhone score pad for the card game Spades. Enter each player’s bid and the tricks they took. The app scores made bids, sets, bags, bag penalties, nil and blind nil for both teams.

  • Undo any submit, and a full history of every hand
  • Set your own house rules: points to win, bag penalty, nil values
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Works offline.
