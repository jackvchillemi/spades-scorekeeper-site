Help for Spades Scorekeeper: Bid & Bag

Need help, found a bug, or want a rule added? Email support@aldo-coach.app. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn’t working.

How do I enter a hand?

Before the hand, tap the box under each player and pick their bid, then tap Submit Bids. After the hand, tap each box again, enter the tricks they took, and tap Submit Results. Tricks must add up to 13.

How do I bid nil or blind nil?

When picking a bid, choose Nil or Blind Nil. Enter that player’s tricks as normal after the hand. The app scores the nil for you.

I made a mistake. How do I fix it?

Tap Undo at the top right. Each tap steps back one submit, so you can go back and fix bids or tricks. Before you submit, just tap a box again to change it. Undo covers scores only; player names and settings are not undone.

What does “Available Bags” mean?

It is 13 minus every bid on the table: the number of tricks nobody bid for. Tricks a team takes over its bid are bags, worth 1 point each. Collect too many and the team loses points.

Do a nil bidder’s tricks count for their partner?

By default, yes: if a nil bidder takes tricks, those tricks count toward their partner’s bid, and any extras are bags. Many groups play the other way. Turn off Nil tricks count for partner in Settings to ignore them.

Can I change the scoring rules?

Yes. Tap the gear icon to set points to win, how many bags trigger a penalty and how big it is, and what nil and blind nil are worth. Changes apply to the next hand you score. Hands already scored keep their points.

Where is my game saved?

On your iPhone only. The game saves after every change, so you can close the app and come back. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I start over?

Tap New Game at the top left. Player names and settings stay. The previous game is kept so you can Undo it. To erase everything, delete the app.
