Spades Scorekeeper

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy covers the Spades Scorekeeper: Bid & Bag app for iPhone (“the app”), published by River Lentil LLC (“we”, “us”).

The short version

The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any information about you. Everything you type into it is kept on your phone (and in any iPhone backups you make). It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

To keep your game going between uses, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • The player names you type in
  • Bids, tricks, scores and the hand-by-hand history of the current game
  • Earlier game states, including previous games, so that Undo can go back to them (up to 200 steps)
  • Your scoring settings

The app never sends this data to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. It may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which are controlled by you and Apple.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party code that collects data
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data
  • No internet connection is used by the app

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Data stays on your phone until you delete the app. Tapping New Game starts a fresh scoreboard but does not erase the previous game: it is kept so that Undo can bring it back. The oldest saved steps are dropped once there are more than 200.

To remove everything, delete the app: touch and hold its icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Copies in iPhone backups you made earlier stay until those backups are deleted.

Your choices and rights

The app does not ask for your consent to collect anything, because it collects nothing. We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, delete, sell or share. You control the data on your device and can erase it at any time as described above. Wherever you live, including the EU, UK and California, you can contact us at the address below with any privacy question or request.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Apple

Apple may collect information when you download the app or use the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us crash reports and overall usage statistics that do not identify you. We do not receive your name, contact details, or anything you enter in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the App Store listing first.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@aldo-coach.app
